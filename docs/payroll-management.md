# Payroll roster

The Salary Management roster separates active users from payroll eligibility.
Only employees marked **Included** appear in the salary-slip employee selector
or are considered by automatic monthly salary generation. Use the **Not
included** view to add eligible active employees or exclude someone. Excluding
an employee does not delete or change previously generated salary slips.
Inactive employees are not shown in either roster view. Deactivating an
employee also removes them from payroll; reactivating them does not
automatically restore payroll eligibility.

Existing users with a salary structure or salary-slip history are included by
the payroll-roster migration, except `SUPER_ADMIN` accounts. Those accounts
start excluded because administrative access does not imply employee payroll
eligibility. HR or an administrator can explicitly change the setting.

## Department and job title

Department describes an organizational function; designation describes a
person's title. Use designation for titles such as Manager, Managing Director,
or Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), rather than adding each title as a department.
The profile already supports a free-text designation.

The current department choices are fixed. If the organization expects to add
and rename departments regularly, replace the enum with an administrator-
managed department list. Reasonable functional departments to consider are
Finance, Customer Success, Product, IT, Content, and Executive Management.
For a small team, prefer a concise list of actual teams and keep titles in
designation.

## Salary Management PIN

HR and administrators must set an individual six-digit PIN before opening
Salary Management. PINs are stored as salted, one-way scrypt hashes; they are
not reversibly encrypted and do not replace account authentication or role
permissions. Five incorrect attempts temporarily lock PIN verification for
15 minutes.

An unlocked browser session is cleared when its browser session ends and locks
after five minutes without pointer, keyboard, touch, or scroll activity.
Protected server actions check the signed, HTTP-only unlock cookie as well as
the user's existing salary permissions. Salary slip views and PDF exports use
the same PIN session.

Managers can request a single-use reset link sent to their account email. The
link expires after 30 minutes and changes only the salary PIN, not the account
password. Reset requests are limited to one per minute.

## Review, approve, and email salary slips

The scheduled monthly job continues to generate base salary slips as before
and emails each newly generated PDF to the employee's account email. If PDF
delivery fails, the job reports an error; rerunning the job retries delivery
for slips generated by the scheduler that have not yet been emailed. It does
not add one-time incentives or deductions.

For a specific employee and month, open the generated slip's adjustment
control to add one-time earnings or deductions with a description. These
entries affect only that slip, are recorded with the HR user who added them,
and appear as separate lines in the final PDF. Use recurring salary structure
components for ongoing compensation. Deductions should be reviewed against
applicable employment and payroll rules.

If HR makes a one-time adjustment after the scheduled email, use **Approve**
after reviewing the updated slip. Approval locks the amounts; adjustments can
no longer be changed. HR can then manually choose **Email PDF** to send the
updated approved slip to the employee's account email. Successful delivery is
recorded and the action can be repeated to resend.
Marking a slip **Paid** is a separate step that records the actual payment.
Approval is recorded separately from the database status enum so deployments
do not need ownership privileges to alter the existing PostgreSQL enum.
